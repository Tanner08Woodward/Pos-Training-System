The register Practice →

  • Left: the order. Every item you ring shows here with its price. Subtotal, tax and total are at the bottom.
  • Middle: the buttons for the tab you're on.
  • Right: the tabs (Main Menu, Cones and Dishes, Pints and Quarts...). PAY / CLOSE ORDER is the big green button at the bottom.
  • Top: New Order, Discount, Manager (Delete Item / Delete All).
Flavors are never rung. You ring the size and what it's served in. You call the flavor out to the scooper.

Sizes: count the flavors Practice →

1 flavor = Small. 2 flavors = Medium.
Unless the guest says a size, then ring what they said. Large also holds up to 2 flavors (bigger scoops).
"Can I get a cake cone of strawberry?"
Ring: Small Cone
"A cup with vanilla and chocolate."
Ring: Medium Cup
"A waffle of coffee and graham."
Ring: Medium Waffle Cone
"A LARGE waffle bowl of mint."
Ring: Large Waffle Bowl

Cones, cups & dipped Practice →

  • Sugar cone or cake cone → the plain Cone buttons (Small / Medium / Large Cone).
  • Cup (dish) → the Cup buttons.
  • Waffle cone / waffle bowl → their own buttons.
  • Dipped waffle cone → TWO lines: the waffle cone, then the brown Dipped button (plain, sprinkles, or peanuts).
  • Kid's cones → on the Cones and Dishes tab.
"Medium waffle cone dipped with sprinkles."
Ring: Medium Waffle ConeDipped Waffle Cone With Sprinkles

Fixing mistakes Practice →

  1. Tap the wrong line(s) on the left to highlight them. You can highlight more than one.
  2. Tap Manager at the top.
  3. Tap Delete Item. Every highlighted line is removed.
  • Nothing highlighted? Delete Item removes the last line.
  • Delete All clears the whole order. Only use it when the guest cancels everything.
  • Guest changes their mind? Delete only what changed, then ring the new item. Don't start over.

Taking payment Practice →

Enter the cash they HAND you, not the total. That's how the register shows the right change.
  • $1 / $5 / $10 / $20: tap the bills you were handed (tap $20 twice for $40).
  • Exact (shows the total): guest paid the exact amount.
  • Nearest: rounds up to the next dollar.
  • Custom $: type any amount (e.g. $50 bill), then Enter.
  • Card, tap or phone pay → Pay with Pinpad.

Discounts Practice →

Ring the order first, then Discount → % Discount → type the percent → OK.

  • 50% off: off-the-clock team members, team members' families, and veterans, military and first responders (police, firefighters, EMTs) and their families.
  • Veterans/military/first responder discount does not apply to pints and quarts.
  • No discounts on gift cards. Discounts can't be combined with coupons.
  • Not sure? Ask your shift lead before applying a discount.

Calling out orders

After ringing, call the order to the scooper: vessel + flavors. The scooper knows the size from the number of flavors.

"Can I get a cake cone strawberry?"
1 flavor → Small
"Can I get a waffle of coffee and graham?"
2 flavors → Medium

Nicknames: "coffee" = Coffee Chocolate Chip · "graham" = Graham Central Station
